What can I use for free?

Daily and weekly Screen Time reports and daily limits for up to eight individual apps are free, with Screen Time permission. Premium is optional and adds scheduled Mindful Pauses.

Does a pause appear before the app opens?

The selected app opens first. A personal automation you set up in Apple Shortcuts then brings PhoneAware forward for the pause. You can choose Not now or Continue.

Are widgets and Live Activities available everywhere?

They require enhanced Screen Time access. Your device must be in the EU, and your Apple Account country or region must also be in the EU. Buying Premium does not grant this access.
