# PhoneAware Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

PhoneAware is designed with privacy in mind.

## Screen Time Data

PhoneAware uses Apple's Screen Time technologies to display information about your device usage.

This information is processed locally on your device.

PhoneAware does not transmit your Screen Time or device usage information to our servers.

## Data Collection

PhoneAware does not require an account.

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal Screen Time or device usage information.

We do not use your device usage information for advertising or tracking.

## Purchases

PhoneAware may offer optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed by Apple. PhoneAware does not receive or store your payment information.
